# Provenance

entries in `decisions.md` it relies on.

- **Decisions:** none.

## Rules purity check
- **Files:** `tools/purity.js`, `tools/walk.js`, `test/purity.test.js`
- **Spec:** 12 Every machine plays the same game (nothing in the rules
  is random; results mustn't depend on the machine); CLAUDE.md,
  Architecture requirements (no Phaser or DOM in the rules).
- **What it checks:** code in `src/rules/` and `src/mapgen/` imports
  only files inside those folders, and doesn't use `Math.random`, Math
  functions that engines may compute differently, `**`, time, or
  browser, Node or Phaser globals. `Math.sqrt` is allowed: it's
  exactly rounded everywhere.
- **Decisions:** none. The map generator counts as rules code, because
  clients may rebuild a generated map from what the host sends
  (spec 02, Generated maps).

## Computer players
- **Files:** `src/ai/personalities.js`, `src/ai/belief.js`,
  `src/ai/aim.js`, `src/ai/value.js`, `src/ai/candidates.js`,
  `src/ai/land.js`, `src/ai/brain.js`; `src/client/core/computers.js`,
  `src/client/core/session.js` (thinking in slices); `tools/ai-match.js`
  (computer against computer, for checking).
- **Spec:** 09 (all of it); 12 Computer players (the personality
  choice); 01 (the flight model it aims with).
- **Decisions:** D43, D44, D45, D48 (crossing water), D55 (a Pontoon aimed
  at a wet point of a tile that is only partly water), D58 (bridging
  only water that's in the way), D59 (watching every turn, and
  answering attacks; `src/server/net-game.js` and the session let
  computers watch each tick of a flight), D64 (each personality's
  level, shown in the setup).
- **Known gaps** (spec 09 not yet covered):
  - **Planning ahead:** apart from crossing water (D48), it looks one
    move ahead; it doesn't plan a chain of Outposts toward a goal, or
    save a round ahead for a particular level 7 launch.
  - **Pontoons with poor aim:** the easier personalities often miss
    the tile they meant, and lay many Pontoons before they cross, if
    they do.
  - **Bait:** it doesn't fire a cheap shot to draw an Interceptor's
    missile and attack on its next turn.
  - **Scouting with shots:** it doesn't use Scatter Shells or Charges
    to look into the fog.
  - **Its own Rovers:** it doesn't avoid leaving them parked against
    its own buildings, or keep a lane open for them.

- **Known gaps** (to come back to):
  - **Not yet listened to.** It has been checked in a headless browser
    (sounds start, follow and stop; songs change) but not by ear. The
    recipes, the levels and the music's layers need a listening pass.
  - **The aide's voices** depend on the voices the player's system has.
    They're chosen by name on macOS, iOS, Chrome and Windows; Android's
    voice names say nothing of who speaks, so there the choice is
    rough. The user wants another pass on the voices. Recordings (D50)
    would settle it.
  - **The game menu's sliders** change the volume at once but play no
    sample on release (the spec asks this of the Options screen only).

- **Decisions:** D52, D56 (numbered repeat names; a duplicated tab starts

- **Known gaps** (to come back to):
  - **Rejoining after a disconnect** isn't offered (the spec makes
    rejoining optional).
  - **No accounts or security:** anyone who can reach the server can
    list and join its games. It's meant for friends on a network.
  - **A setup screen chat line** sent just as a player joins can be
    missed by them.
  - **Check 2 across devices** hasn't been run on different browsers
    and machines yet, only two headless Chromes on one machine.
